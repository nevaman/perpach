PROJECT CONTEXT

Evidence first.

Site work has established an early local operation. The next stage is not built on unsupported resource or return claims: it begins with independent review, controlled production records, gold accounting and clear operating responsibilities.

Legal-rights language, workforce numbers, equipment arrangements and technical results will be published only after their sources, effective dates and professional approvals are complete.

What distinguishes Lunga is not its scale — it is the discipline with which the next phase is being approached. Every decision point requires evidence. Every expansion step requires verification of the previous one.

SITE OPERATIONS / EQUIPMENT
DEVELOPMENT PATHWAY

Four controlled steps.

  1. 01 / VerifyIndependent inspection, baseline data collection, geological sampling programme, and reconciliation of all historical production records. Third-party technical review of site conditions, equipment status and environmental baseline.
  2. 02 / StabiliseImplement formal safety management system, production tracking protocols, fuel and consumables reconciliation, equipment-hours logging, and chain-of-custody documentation for all gold output. Establish clear operating responsibilities.
  3. 03 / ExpandMilestone-based capacity decisions supported by operating evidence. Expansion only proceeds when verification and stabilisation metrics are met. Capital deployment tied to demonstrated performance.
  4. 04 / ExploreA professionally designed exploration programme targeting primary-gold potential within and adjacent to the current operation. Geological mapping, sampling and geophysical survey work guided by qualified professionals.

THE OPPORTUNITY

Why Lunga matters.

Ethiopia's gold sector represents one of the most significant untapped opportunities in African mining. Lunga sits within a geological province that has attracted international attention but remains largely under-explored by modern standards.

What Perpach brings is not a speculative land position — it is an established operating presence, local relationships, regulatory familiarity and a founder-led commitment to building value the right way.

What remains to be established

Mining is inherently uncertain. Material work includes confirming rights wording, technical interpretation, recovery performance, costs, environmental obligations, community context and the commercial basis for expansion.

We do not minimise these uncertainties — we organise our development pathway around resolving them systematically. Qualified partners receive controlled information through a reviewed diligence process.
