OUR FRAMEWORK

Honest reporting on what matters.

We organise our responsibility commitments into four pillars. For each, we distinguish between what is currently implemented, what is under development, and what is planned for future phases.

People & safety

Induction protocols, personal protective equipment standards, incident recording systems, training programmes and emergency preparedness plans. Our goal is zero harm — our reporting covers the gap between that goal and current reality.

Environment

Water management, fuel storage and handling, waste segregation, land disturbance tracking and rehabilitation planning. Controls are scaled to each operational stage and upgraded as capacity grows.

Communities

Consistent community engagement, local employment priority, transparent grievance mechanisms and a safe, confidential route for concerns. We work within communities, not around them.

Ethics & compliance

Anti-bribery controls, conflicts of interest management, responsible gold sales verification, evidence integrity protocols and regulatory compliance tracking. These are non-negotiable.

PEOPLE & SAFETY / ON SITE
IMPLEMENTATION STATUS

Where we stand today.

We believe the mining industry suffers from too many aspirational commitments and too few honest assessments of current practice. Perpach takes a different approach: we report what is implemented, acknowledge what is incomplete, and describe what comes next.

This means our responsibility disclosures may look less polished than those of larger companies. That is intentional. We would rather be honest about our current stage than publish commitments we cannot yet demonstrate.

As the business develops and professional management structures are established, our responsibility framework will be independently reviewed and benchmarked against recognised mining industry standards.

COMMUNITY / LOCAL ENGAGEMENT
COMMUNITY COMMITMENT

Built on local relationships.

Our community engagement is not a corporate programme bolted onto operations — it is woven into how we work. Local knowledge, local hiring and local accountability are foundational principles, not afterthoughts.

We maintain open communication channels with community leaders, provide regular updates on operational activities, and operate a confidential grievance mechanism accessible to all community members.
