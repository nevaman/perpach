EDITORIAL POLICY

How we report.

All newsroom content is reviewed and approved by Perpach management before publication. We distinguish between facts (verified, sourced) and forward-looking statements (clearly labelled, qualified).

If a material inaccuracy is identified in any published update, we will publish a dated correction in this newsroom and update the original item. We do not quietly edit past statements — corrections are transparent and permanent.

This newsroom is not a promotional channel. It exists to provide stakeholders with an accurate, dated record of Perpach's activities and decisions.
