THE ETHIOPIAN HIGHLANDS
OUR STORY

Several years of ground-level work.

Perpach was founded with a simple thesis: that an Ethiopian-based operator with genuine local knowledge could build a mining business worth investing in — if it was willing to do the hard, unglamorous work of establishing an evidence base first.

Several years of practical site work have established a real operating foundation and the lessons needed for the next phase. The early period taught us how to operate in a challenging environment, build local relationships, navigate regulatory requirements and maintain safety standards with limited infrastructure.

Today, Perpach is entering its next chapter. Ownership oversight and daily operations are being separated as professional controls, technical capacity and strategic partnerships develop. This is not a pivot — it is the plan we set out to execute.

OUR TRANSITION

From founder-led to professionally managed.

The next phase of Perpach requires capabilities that go beyond what any single founder can provide. We are deliberately building a leadership structure that brings in experienced mining professionals while retaining the local knowledge and relationships that make our operations possible.

This transition includes formal separation of governance and operations, introduction of independent technical oversight, and a structured approach to strategic partnership that prioritises alignment over speed.

OUR PRINCIPLES

Non-negotiable standards.

These are not aspirations. They are the operating principles we hold ourselves to today — and the basis on which we invite scrutiny.

Evidence over narrative

We do not publish claims we cannot support. Where data is incomplete, we say so. Where work remains, we describe it honestly.

Local capability

Ethiopian talent and knowledge are not secondary inputs — they are the foundation. International expertise complements; it does not replace.

Staged commitment

We earn the right to expand by proving we can operate well at the current scale. No milestone is skipped.

Transparent reporting

Material corrections are recorded publicly. Implementation status is distinguished from future intent in all communications.
